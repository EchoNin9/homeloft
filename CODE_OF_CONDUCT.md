# Code of Conduct

Homeloft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere
else the project lives.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer, [adamjinks](https://github.com/adamjinks), privately
instead of replying in public. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and
warn or ban anyone who breaks this code, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
